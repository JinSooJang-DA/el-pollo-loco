Chicken Combat

Stomp chickens or throw bottles.

Baby chicks are too cute to stomp! Use bottles instead.

Health & Coins

Coins restore 10 health. At full health they are stored and protect you from damage.

One coin per level hangs too high for a normal jump. Stomp the boss to boost up and grab it – it restores your health to 100%!

Boss Battles

Wait 3 seconds between successful attacks.

Sombreros & Rewards

Finish levels 2–5 with full health to earn a Sombrero.

A Sombrero gives you an extra life when Pepe falls.

Collect 3 or more Sombreros or 20 or more coins to unlock special ending rewards.
